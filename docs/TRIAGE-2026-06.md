## Classification legend

- **actionable-now** — well-specified, reproducible, fixable without more input.
- **needs-author-info** — under-specified / not reproducible as written.
- **needs-verification** — labelled bug but empty/thin body; must reproduce first.
- **wontfix-candidate** — out of scope, niche, or maintainer has expressed doubt.
- **duplicate** — covered by another issue/PR.
- **already-fixed** — appears resolved on `main` or by an open PR.
- **has-PR** — an open PR already targets it.
